## A. Detailed (academic) deck: 4 slides

### Slide 1. Prototype overview

**Title:** A clickable prototype of the Relore app

**On-slide text (28 words):**
> Ten screens covering buying, selling, support and the Relore team's listing review. Built in the group's brand, with every fee worked out live from one set of rules.

**Screenshots to place:**
- Main image, large, left or centre: `11-desktop-phone-frame.png`
- Two smaller phone screens beside it: `01-splash.png` and `02-home-browse.png`

**Speaker notes (88 words):**
This is our clickable prototype. It runs in a browser on a laptop or a phone, and every screen can be reached by tapping through. It covers the buyer journey from browsing to tracking an order, the seller journey from photos to payout, a help page, and the Relore team's review queue. All amounts come from one set of functions: a twenty per cent commission, and a delivery fee of QAR 15 plus QAR 0.80 per kilometre. The listings are sample data, and no real payments are taken.

### Slide 2. Key user flows

**Title:** Two journeys: buying and selling

**On-slide text (22 words):**
> **Buyer:** browse reviewed listings → item details → chat → checkout → track the order.
> **Seller:** four guided photos → item details → review by Relore → receive 80%.

**Screenshots to place (two rows of three, in flow order with arrows between them):**
- Buyer row: `03a-item-details.png` → `05a-checkout-delivery-fee.png` → `06b-order-tracking-out-for-delivery.png`
- Seller row: `07a-sell-step1-photo-guides.png` → `07d-sell-step2-price-and-payout.png` → `08b-seller-dashboard-earnings-breakdown.png`

**Speaker notes (89 words):**
There are two main journeys. A buyer browses listings that Relore has reviewed, opens an item to see four photos, the measurements and the condition, and can chat with the seller. At checkout the delivery fee is worked out from the road distance: twelve kilometres costs QAR 24.60. The buyer tracks the order until a Relore driver delivers it. A seller adds four guided photos, enters the details and sees the payout before submitting. Once approved, it goes live, and the seller receives eighty per cent of the sale.

### Slide 3. MVP testing mind-map

**Title:** How we will test Relore's MVPs

**On-slide text (24 words):**
> Seven ways to test, from a concierge MVP on Instagram and WhatsApp to a campus pilot, plus what we measure and when we pivot.

**Image to place:**
- Full width: `mindmap/mindmap.png` (or `mindmap.pdf` for the sharpest result). Keep it as large as the slide allows so the branches stay readable when projected.

**Speaker notes (89 words):**
We will test cheaply before building anything expensive. A concierge MVP takes orders by hand through Instagram and WhatsApp. A Wizard of Oz MVP looks like a finished app while we review listings and arrange delivery manually. We will run task-based usability tests of this prototype with QU students, a landing-page waitlist that ties in with Part 5, and A/B tests on Instagram and TikTok. A campus pilot and follow-up interviews come next. The last two branches show the metrics we track and our pivot-or-persevere thresholds, which are assumptions.

### Slide 4. MVP metrics

**Title:** What we measure, and when we change course

**On-slide text (29 words):**
> **Five metrics:** listings submitted · share of listings sold · repeat buyers · delivery cost per order · review turnaround time.
> **Go or pivot:** thresholds are assumptions the group still has to confirm.

**Screenshots to place:**
- `10-admin-review-queue.png` (where review turnaround time is measured)
- `08a-seller-dashboard.png` (listing statuses: Awaiting review, Live, Sold)
- A crop of the two dark branches of `mindmap/mindmap.png` ("Metrics to track" and "Pivot or persevere criteria")

**Speaker notes (89 words):**
Each metric answers one question. Listings submitted shows whether sellers will supply stock, the main risk in our survey. Share of listings sold shows whether prices and demand match. Repeat buyers shows trust. Delivery cost per order must stay below the fee we charge. Review turnaround time shows whether hand review can keep up. Our draft thresholds are thirty orders a month by month three, thirty per cent of listings sold within thirty days, and a quarter of buyers ordering again. These are assumptions for the group to agree.

## Checklist before presenting

- The word "driver" always means a Relore driver employed by the company, never a team member.
- Commission is 20% everywhere. The older group deck still says 10% on two slides (see the README).
- Declined items: the buyer gets a full refund including the delivery fee, which is taken from the seller's next payout (Relore covers it if there is none). Say this if asked.
- The delivery fee can be paid by card, Apple Pay or Fawran, and is always paid before a driver is sent.
