# Relore: two-minute pitching video script

**Audience:** investors. **Length:** 2:00. **Spoken words:** 281 (about 140 words a minute, a calm presenting pace).
**Speakers:** Speaker 1 to Speaker 5. Replace these with names once the group has agreed who says what.

The script describes the future, investor-funded business. Drivers are always "Relore drivers" or "our own drivers" (employees of the company), never the team.

### Where each number comes from

| Line | Source |
|---|---|
| "Three in four … unclear condition" | Market feasibility document, survey (n = 29): unclear condition 75.9%, 22 of 29. |
| "Reviews and clear photos are what buyers trust most" | Same survey: reviews 75.9%, clear photos 65.5%. |
| "Almost all … use Instagram or TikTok daily" | Same survey: 96.6%, 28 of 29. |
| "1.3 billion dollars … triple by 2035" | Future Market Insights, GCC second-hand apparel: $1.3B (2025) to $4B (2035). A vendor estimate, so keep the word "estimated". |
| "About two million Gen Z and Millennial residents" | Feasibility document: about 2.1M (67.1% of 3.19M). The document says to treat this as an upper limit. |
| "Twenty per cent" and "fifteen riyals plus eighty dirhams per kilometre" | Group decision of 13 November: 20% commission, QAR 15 + QAR 0.80 per km. |
| "About nine orders a day" with one driver | BMC PDF, page 12: break-even with 1 driver is 9 orders a day. |

Two things are deliberately **not** said, because the group's own data does not support them: that the survey proves people accept the fees (it suggests most would not, see the README), and any specific investment amount (not yet agreed).

## Filming tips

**Recording on a phone**
- Film in landscape (16:9) at 1080p, 30 frames a second. Use the rear camera, not the selfie camera.
- Rest the phone on a tripod or a stack of books at eye level. Lock focus and exposure by pressing and holding on the speaker's face.
- Record sound with wired earphones or a second phone held close, out of shot, using a voice memo app. Clap once at the start so the sound can be lined up.
- Leave two seconds of silence before and after each line. It makes cutting much easier.

**Lighting**
- Face a window in daylight. Never stand with the window behind you.
- Use the same spot and time of day for all five speakers so the shots match.
- Switch off overhead lights if they cause a yellow tint or flicker.

**Screen recording the prototype**
- Open `prototype/index.html?clean=1` so the "Demo screens" handle is hidden.
- On a laptop, record the phone frame with the built-in recorder (Windows: Win + Alt + R, or the Snipping Tool's record mode. Mac: Shift + Cmd + 5). On a phone, open the file in the browser and use the built-in screen recorder.
- Rehearse the taps first. Move slowly and pause for a second on each screen.
- Reload the page before each take so the sample data starts fresh.

**Editing in Canva**
- Use only the supplied logo files. Do not stretch, recolour or add shadows to them, and keep them on Supermoon, white, Relore Maroon or After Hours.
- Captions and graphics: Jost, After Hours (#38373D) on Supermoon (#EEEFEA), accents in Relore Maroon (#8A1538). Modern Love (#ECCEC4) and Ryokan Guesthouse (#DDCAB9) work as soft backgrounds for cards. Never put text on 36 Hours in Marrakesh (#C9877B).
- Add subtitles. Many people watch without sound.
- Rehearse with `teleprompter.html` until the full read takes two minutes.
